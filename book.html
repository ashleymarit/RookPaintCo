Work a wall.

Send the wall. Size, photos, timing. We sketch after we have seen it. This page is a studio demo — the number does not ring.

  1. 1

    The wall

    Photos in daylight, a rough width and height, and whether it is outside. Note sun, neighbors, and anything that cannot be painted over.

  2. 2

    A pass

    We send a color and type sketch. One revision is part of the job. The wall decides the rest once we are on the ladder.

  3. 3

    Paint days

    Weather window, access, and a start hour. We bring the cans. You keep the wall clear.
