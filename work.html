Lookbook

The walls.

Five looks. Palette, surface, and the weather that sits on them.

01 Tide Line 02 Stencil Break 03 Ochre Field 04 Night Can 05 Fog Cap

Stylist breakdown

Look 01

Tide Line

Horizontal bands stacked like tide marks — seafoam, navy, sand — with overspray where the can did not lift clean.

Palette

  • Cream
  • Cyan
  • Night
  • Rust
  • Fog

Surface

Exterior stucco, salt-weathered.

Caps

Horizontal bands. Fat pass, then a thin edge.

Wear

Salt air. The bands have to hold from a moving car.

Stylist breakdown

Look 02

Stencil Break

A hard stencil rectangle, then the bleed that happens when the card lifts too soon.

Palette

  • Cream
  • Magenta
  • Acid
  • Night

Surface

Interior gypsum, primed.

Caps

Stencil plus bleed. One fat drip off the frame.

Light

Work-light on a shop wall. The break reads as a pass, not a mistake.

Stylist breakdown

Look 03

Ochre Field

A warm coast field — rust ground, fog overspray, cream and acid drips that settle like weather.

Palette

  • Rust
  • Fog
  • Cream
  • Acid

Surface

Brick, lime-washed.

Caps

Color field. Three drip runs, left to right.

Wear

Afternoon sun. Ochre still holds when the fog sits on it.

Stylist breakdown

Look 04

Night Can

Black ground so the magenta and acid can read as light, not paint.

Palette

  • Night
  • Magenta
  • Acid

Surface

Shop roll-up, mill finish.

Caps

Fat cap. Two long drips, no fill.

Light

Night work-light. No ambient, no daylight pass.

Stylist breakdown

Look 05

Fog Cap

Grey field, cyan overspray, cream drips — the wall after the marine layer sits on it.

Palette

  • Fog
  • Cyan
  • Cream

Surface

Stucco, north-facing.

Caps

Soft field. Thin drips, no hard edge.

Wear

Morning fog. The paint never quite dries the same twice.
